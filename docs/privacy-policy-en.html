🔒 Privacy Policy

NotBad - Wrong Answer Notebook | Last Updated: December 1, 2025

Our Commitment: NotBad is a privacy-focused, local-first application. Your data stays on your device. We do not collect, upload, or share any of your personal information.

1. Data Collection

Data stored by NotBad:

  • Photos: Photos you take or select are stored only on your device
  • Learning metadata: Subject, difficulty, knowledge points, mastery level
  • Review records: Review timestamps and mastery ratings

We DO NOT collect:

  • Personal identification information (name, phone, email)
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers
  • Usage analytics

2. Data Usage

Your data is used only to:

  • Display and manage your wrong answer entries
  • Calculate review reminders based on the Ebbinghaus forgetting curve
  • Generate personal learning statistics

We DO NOT:

  • Use data for advertising
  • Use data for user profiling
  • Sell data to third parties

3. Data Storage

  • Local Storage: All data is stored locally on your iPhone/iPad by default
  • iCloud Sync (Optional): If enabled, data syncs via Apple iCloud across your devices. This data is encrypted by Apple; we cannot access it

4. Data Sharing

We DO NOT share your data with any third parties.

The app does not include:

  • Third-party advertising SDKs
  • Analytics SDKs
  • Social sharing SDKs
  • Any tracking code

5. Data Security

  • Data is stored in iOS sandbox environment, inaccessible to other apps
  • If device passcode/Face ID is enabled, data is protected by device encryption
  • iCloud data uses end-to-end encryption

6. Data Deletion

You can delete your data at any time:

  • Delete single entry: Swipe left in the library
  • Delete all data: Settings → Data Management → Clear All Data
  • Uninstall App: Removes all local data

7. Permissions

Permissions requested and their purposes:

  • Camera: To take photos of wrong answers, used only when you actively take a photo
  • Photo Library: To select existing photos, accesses only photos you choose

These permissions are optional. You can disable them in system settings at any time.

8. Children's Privacy

NotBad is suitable for users of all ages. We do not intentionally collect personal information from children under 13. Since the app does not collect any personal information, parents can allow their children to use it with confidence.

9. Policy Updates

If we update this privacy policy, we will post the new version on this page with an updated date. Significant changes will be notified within the app.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us:

  • Email: notbad.app@outlook.com
