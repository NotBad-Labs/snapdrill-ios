## 应用描述

### 中文 (简体)

**NotBad 不错错题本** - 让错题不再重犯

📸 **拍照即可记录**
告别手抄错题的繁琐。拍照、从相册选择，或使用实况文本智能识别，一键保存错题。

🧠 **科学复习提醒**
基于艾宾浩斯遗忘曲线，智能安排复习时间。在最容易遗忘的时刻提醒你复习，让记忆更持久。

📚 **分类管理，一目了然**
按学科、难度、题型、来源多维度分类。支持 14 个学科，覆盖小学到高中全学段。

🎴 **卡片式复习**
滑动卡片，标记掌握程度。左滑「需加强」，右滑「已掌握」，让复习像刷短视频一样轻松。

📊 **数据看板**
可视化学习进度，了解各科目掌握情况，发现薄弱知识点。

🔐 **隐私优先**
所有数据存储在本地，不上传任何信息。你的学习记录，只属于你。

---

**适合人群**
• 中小学生：记录课堂、作业、考试中的错题
• 高中生：系统整理知识漏洞，备战高考
• 家长：帮助孩子建立错题复习习惯

**免费无广告**
核心功能完全免费，没有广告干扰，专注学习体验。

立即下载，让每一道错题都变成进步的阶梯！

### English

**NotBad - Study Notes** - Learn from Your Mistakes

📸 **Capture in Seconds**
No more tedious handwriting. Take a photo, pick from your album, or use Live Text recognition to save mistakes instantly.

🧠 **Smart Review Reminders**
Based on the Ebbinghaus forgetting curve, NotBad schedules reviews at optimal times to strengthen your memory.

📚 **Organized by Category**
Sort by subject, difficulty, question type, and source. Supports 14 subjects across elementary to high school.

🎴 **Card-Based Review**
Swipe through flashcards and mark your mastery. Swipe left for "Need Practice", right for "Mastered".

📊 **Progress Dashboard**
Visualize your learning progress, identify weak areas, and track improvement over time.

🔐 **Privacy First**
All data is stored locally on your device. No cloud uploads, no tracking. Your study records belong to you.

---

**Perfect for**
• K-12 Students: Record mistakes from class, homework, and exams
• High School Students: Systematically organize knowledge gaps
• Parents: Help children build effective study habits

**Free & Ad-Free**
Core features are completely free with no ads. Focus on learning, not distractions.

Download now and turn every mistake into a stepping stone for success!
