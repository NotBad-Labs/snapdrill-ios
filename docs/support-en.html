❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add mistakes?

Tap the "+" button on the home screen. You can add mistakes by taking a photo, selecting from your photo library, or using the live scanner. The app will automatically recognize text in the image.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. All your data is stored locally on your device. We do not collect or upload any personal information. If you enable iCloud sync, data is synced to your own iCloud account through Apple's encrypted channel.

How do I transfer to a new device?

Enable iCloud sync, then sign in with the same Apple ID on your new device. All data will sync automatically.

How are review reminders calculated?

We use the Ebbinghaus forgetting curve algorithm to intelligently calculate the optimal review time based on your mastery level for each mistake. Lower mastery means shorter review intervals.

What languages are supported?

The app supports Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English, Japanese, and Korean. OCR recognition supports Chinese and English text.

📱 System Requirements

NotBad requires iOS 26.0 or later. Compatible with iPhone and iPad.
